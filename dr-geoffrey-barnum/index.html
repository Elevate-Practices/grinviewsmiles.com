Dr. Geoffrey Barnum, DMD

Dentist at Grinview Smiles in Grandview, Texas

Book now(817) 866-2065

Geoff Barnum grew up in southern Idaho and is the third of four children. He attended Idaho State University, where he received his Bachelor’s degree in Biology. He took a leave from his studies to serve a Christian mission in South Korea.

While studying at Idaho State, he met his beautiful wife Becky. They have been married for over 14 years and have 6 children together. He enjoys spending time with his family when he is not in the office.

Dr. Barnum completed his dental school training from Case School of Dental Medicine in Cleveland, Ohio. The family enjoyed their time there but ran away from the bitter winters and have called Texas home since 2011.

Dr. Barnum continues to learn about new dental techniques by taking continuing education courses so he can provide the highest quality of care to his patients.

Dr. Barnum was very involved in athletics and outdoor activities as a youth and continues to enjoy these today. He enjoys frequenting all the local restaurants and can’t get enough Tex-Mex!

Now welcoming new patients ages 7 and up.
