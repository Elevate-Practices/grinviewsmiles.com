The twice-a-year visit that keeps small problems small.

Why regular visits matter

Most dental problems, like cavities and gum disease, start small and quiet. A cleaning and exam every six months is how we catch them early, while they're simple and less costly to treat.

A professional cleaning also removes plaque and tartar that brushing and flossing can't fully reach.

What happens at your visit

  • We remove plaque and tartar from your teeth
  • We floss and polish for a clean, bright finish
  • A dentist checks your teeth, gums and bite
  • Digital X-rays when you need them
  • A plain-English talk about anything we find

How often to come in

Most people do best with a visit every six months. If you've had gum disease or lots of cavities, we may suggest coming in more often, and we'll tell you why.

Common questions

How long does a cleaning and exam take?

Most visits take about an hour, a little longer if you need X-rays or it's your first visit.

Does a cleaning hurt?

For most people, no. If your teeth or gums are sensitive, tell us and we'll go gently.

What age do you see?

We see patients ages 7 and up, so kids, parents and grandparents can all come to the same office.

Does insurance cover cleanings?

Most dental plans cover routine cleanings and exams. No insurance? Our membership plan covers them at 100%.
