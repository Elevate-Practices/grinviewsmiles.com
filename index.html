Questions we hear a lot

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. We welcome new patients ages 7 and up. Book online or call (817) 866-2065.

What insurance do you take?

We're in network with Cigna, Delta Dental, Ameritas, Assurant/DHA, Guardian, Humana and Principal, and we accept many other plans out of network. Call us and we'll check your plan.

I don't have insurance. What can I do?

Join the Grinview Smiles Membership Plan: cleanings, exams and X-rays covered 100%, free whitening and 20% off treatment, starting at $329 a year.

Can I get a crown in one visit?

Usually, yes. Our in-office Planmeca system designs and mills most crowns in a single appointment.

What are your hours?

Monday through Thursday, 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM, closed for lunch from 12 to 1. We're closed Friday through Sunday.

Do you help nervous patients?

Yes. We offer nitrous oxide and oral sedation, and we'll go at your pace.
