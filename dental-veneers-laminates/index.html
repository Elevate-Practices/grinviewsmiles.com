What veneers are

Veneers are thin shells of porcelain or resin bonded to the front of your teeth. Porcelain looks a lot like natural enamel and resists stains.

What they can fix

  • Gaps between teeth
  • Deep stains whitening can't lift
  • Chipped, worn or uneven teeth
  • Teeth that are slightly crooked or oddly shaped

One tooth or a whole smile

We can make a single veneer to match your other teeth, or a set to change your whole smile.

Common questions

Do veneers look fake?

Not when they're done well. We match the shape and shade to your face and smile.

How long do veneers last?

Porcelain veneers can last many years with good care and regular checkups.

Do I need to avoid anything?

Avoid biting hard things like ice or pens, and wear a night guard if you grind your teeth.
