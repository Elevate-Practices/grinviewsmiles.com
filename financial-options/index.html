Insurance & financing

In network with Delta Dental, Cigna, Guardian and more, plus financing and a membership plan.

Book now(817) 866-2065

We want great dental care to fit your budget. Here's how you can pay at Grinview Smiles.

Dental insurance

We're in network with these dental insurance plans:

  • Cigna
  • Delta Dental
  • Ameritas
  • Assurant/DHA
  • Guardian
  • Humana
  • Principal

We also accept many other dental plans out of network and file your claim for you. Call (817) 866-2065 and we'll check your plan before your visit.

Financing

We work with outside companies that offer no- and low-interest payment plans, including CareCredit and Compassionate Finance. Ask our team and we'll help you apply.

No insurance?

Our in-office membership plan covers cleanings, exams and X-rays 100% and saves you money on other care.
