CBCT cone beam 3D imaging in Grandview, TX

A 3D view of your teeth, jaw and surrounding structures.

Book now(817) 866-2065

Grinview Smiles offers CBCT 3D Imaging at our office in Grandview, Texas, for patients ages 7 and up from across Johnson County. Call (817) 866-2065 or book online.

What it is

A cone beam CT (CBCT) scan creates a 3D image of your teeth, jaw and nearby areas. It shows more than a regular X-ray, which helps us plan with more precision.

How we use it

  • Planning dental implants
  • Finding and treating root canal problems
  • Looking at the jaw and joints (TMJ)
  • Checking injuries to the teeth and face
  • Planning and tracking orthodontic treatment
  • Spotting teeth that are impacted or unusual

What to expect

You stand or sit still while the machine rotates around your head. It's quick, painless and done right in our office.

Payment options

We're in network with Delta Dental, Cigna, Guardian, Humana and more, file your claim for you, and offer financing through CareCredit and Compassionate Finance.

Common questions

Is a CBCT scan safe?

We use it when the 3D detail helps your care. The scan is quick and focused on your mouth and jaw.

Does everyone need one?

No. We use CBCT for specific needs like implant planning or complex root canals.

How long does the scan take?

The scan itself takes less than a minute.
